Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. Who We Are

Aurum is a personal wealth management application ("the Service") available at aurum-networth.com . The Service is operated for personal use and does not constitute a regulated financial service.

2. Data We Collect

When you use Aurum, we collect and store the following personal data:

  • Identity data: your name, email address, and profile picture (provided via your authentication provider)
  • Preferences: your chosen display currency, locale, and UI theme
  • Financial data: assets, historical value snapshots, categories, and financial targets that you manually enter

We do not collect browsing data, device fingerprints, or analytics beyond what is described above.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the basis of contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — the processing is necessary to provide the Service you have requested — and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for application security and integrity.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and provide access to your account
  • To display your financial data and calculate net worth analytics
  • To apply your currency and locale preferences across the application

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or profiling purposes.

5. Third-Party Processors

We use the following sub-processors to operate the Service:

  • Auth0 (Okta) — identity and authentication management, EU region (aurum-api.eu.auth0.com)
  • Frankfurter API — publicly available exchange rate data; no personal data is transmitted
  • Vercel — frontend hosting (aurum-networth.com)
  • Hetzner — backend and database hosting (api.aurum-networth.com), EU data centre

6. Data Retention

Your personal data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from Profile → Danger Zone , which permanently removes all your data from our systems and from Auth0.

Internal audit logs (such as asset status change records) are automatically purged after 2 years in accordance with the GDPR storage limitation principle (Art. 5(1)(e)).

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data — available directly in the application at any time
  • Rectification — update your name, currency, and locale in Profile settings
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data via Profile → Danger Zone
  • Data portability — download a full JSON export of your data via Profile → Your Data → Export
  • Restrict processing — contact us to request account suspension without deletion

8. Cookies & Local Storage

The application uses localStorage solely to remember your theme preference (dark/light mode) and your GDPR consent.

Our authentication provider Auth0 (Okta) may set strictly necessary cookies to manage your login session and prevent CSRF attacks. These cookies are essential for the Service to function and are not used for tracking or advertising purposes. No third-party analytics or advertising cookies are used.

9. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Authentication tokens are short-lived JWTs issued by Auth0. Database access is restricted to the application backend.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related requests or questions, please open an issue on the project repository or contact the application owner directly.
